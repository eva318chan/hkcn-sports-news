Hong Kong Women's Asian Games Campaign Ends in Stoppage-Time Penalty Heartbreak

Hong Kong's women fell 1-2 to Uzbekistan in their final Asian Games group match on September 21. Substitute 魏嵐 levelled in the 66th minute after a first-half deficit, but goalkeeper 吳卓蔚 conceded a stoppage-time penalty that sealed the defeat. Three straight losses, following 1-5 to China and 0-2 to the Philippines, ended Hong Kong's campaign without a point.
