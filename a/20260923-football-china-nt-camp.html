China Complete 28-Man Training Squad as Teenager Huang Shenghao Earns Late Call-Up

China's national team finally assembled its full 28-man training squad on September 22 after the three Zhejiang FC internationals reported for duty in Chongqing. 塞尔吉尼奥 missed the camp through injury, 巴頓 withdrew hurt and goalkeeper 蹇韜 returned to Chengdu Rongcheng, with 19-year-old Guangzhou Leopards midfielder 黃晟豪 called up as cover. The squad is preparing for four international friendlies in Chongqing starting September 24.
