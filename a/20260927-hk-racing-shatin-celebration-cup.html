Derby Hero Invincible Ibis Grinds Out Neck Victory in Sha Tin Celebration Cup

The 2026 Hong Kong Derby winner Invincible Ibis resumed with a neck victory in the HK$4.2 million Group 3 Celebration Cup Handicap (1400m) at Sha Tin on Sunday, holding off Galaxy Patch with Stormy Grove a further neck away in third. It was the Hellbent gelding's sixth win from 12 starts and his first Group race success, with Luke Ferraris deputising for the injured Hugh Bowman. Trainer Mark Newnham plans to target either the Hong Kong Mile or Hong Kong Cup in December.
