National Women's Volleyball Championship Day Two: Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Shanghai and Guangdong All Win

Day two of the 正野杯 National Women's Volleyball Championship in Foshan, Guangdong saw four matches decided: Jiangsu swept Hebei 3-0, Guangdong edged Fujian 3-2 in a five-set thriller, Zhejiang blanked Jiangxi 3-0 and Shanghai beat Liaoning 3-0. Fourteen teams are competing over eight days at Shunde's Desheng Sports Center.
