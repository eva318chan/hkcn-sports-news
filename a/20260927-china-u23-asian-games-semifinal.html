28-Year Wait Over: China's Young Guns Stun Thailand 3-0 to Reach Asian Games Last Four

China's U23 Asian Games side thrashed Thailand 3-0 in the quarter-final at Toyota Stadium, reaching the Asian Games men's football semi-finals for the first time in 28 years since 1998. Peng Xiao forced an own goal, Hu Hetao lashed in the second and substitute Zhang Yuning sealed it, after coach Antonio boldly switched formation and made four changes to the line-up. China now face three-time champions South Korea on 30 September for a place in the final.
