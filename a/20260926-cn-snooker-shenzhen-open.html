World No.1 Zhao Xintong headlines historic first Shenzhen Open

The 2026 World Snooker Shenzhen Open, the city's first-ever professional ranking event, runs 28 September to 4 October at Bao'an Sports Center. New world No.1 Zhao Xintong, a Shenzhen native and the tournament ambassador, opens against Scott Donaldson, while Ronnie O'Sullivan, Ding Junhui, defending champion Mark Williams, Judd Trump and Mark Selby also star. The £177,000 winner's prize makes it one of the tour's marquee autumn events.
