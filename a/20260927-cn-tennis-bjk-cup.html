Injury-hit Zheng loses as Italy end China's Billie Jean King Cup run in Shenzhen

On 24 September at Shenzhen Bay Sports Center, China lost 1-2 to defending champions Italy in the Billie Jean King Cup quarter-finals. Zhang Shuai thrashed Elisabetta Cocciaretto 6-0, 6-2, but an ill Zheng Qinwen, who took painkillers to play, fell 3-6, 6-7(4) to Jasmine Paolini, and the doubles pair Guo Hanyu/Jiang Xinyu lost 1-2 to Paolini/Sara Errani. The defeat ended Zheng's 20-match winning streak against Italian opponents and China's home campaign.
