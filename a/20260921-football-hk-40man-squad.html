Hong Kong Name 40-Man Preliminary Squad for Asian Cup Qualifier Trip to Singapore

The HKFA announced a 40-man preliminary squad on September 21 for Hong Kong's opening 2027 Asian Cup qualifier away to Singapore. All eight mainland-based CSL players plus China League One duo 余在言 and 吳宇曦 were selected, while injured Kitchee midfielder 陳俊樂 also made the cut. The squad gathers on October 17, plays a home friendly on the 19th and departs for Singapore on the 21st ahead of the match on the 25th.
