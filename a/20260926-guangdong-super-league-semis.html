Guangzhou Denied by Shenzhen in 1-1 Stalemate as Dongguan Grab Semi-Final Edge

The 2026 Guangdong City Football Super League semi-finals kicked off on 26 September, with Guangzhou drawing 1-1 at home to previously unbeaten Shenzhen at Yuexiushan Stadium. Ye Hongsen's 61st-minute header gave Guangzhou the lead before Zhang Yunbo equalised from a corner ten minutes from time. In the other tie, Dongguan won 1-0 away at Meizhou thanks to an early second-half strike, taking a precious advantage into the 3 October return legs.
