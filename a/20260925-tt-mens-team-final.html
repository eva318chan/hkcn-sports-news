End of an Era: Japan Stuns China 3-2 to Snap 32-Year Asian Games Men's Team Reign

Japan pulled off one of the biggest upsets in Asian Games table tennis history, beating China 3-2 in the men's team final on September 24 to claim the gold for the first time in 60 years. Tomokazu Harimoto starred with two wins, including a stunning 3-0 sweep of world No.1 Wang Chuqin, ending China's eight-title winning streak. Taiwan took the bronze medal for the fourth consecutive Games.
