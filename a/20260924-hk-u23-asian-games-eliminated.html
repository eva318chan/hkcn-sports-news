From Semi-Final Heroes to Last Place: Hong Kong U23 End Asian Games Bottom of the Pile

Hong Kong's U23 men's football team, surprise semi-finalists three years ago, crashed out of the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games after losing all three group matches, finishing 15th and last among the 15 teams. Stand-in head coach Tsang Chiu-tat, who took over after the original coach jumped ship, said the players had given everything but admitted there was a gap to Asian standards. Four senior players return home to join the senior national team for the ASEAN Cup, while Tsang said he was willing to be the 'human shield' for his players amid criticism.
