Zheng Qinwen headlines China Open wildcard list as Beijing field takes shape

The 2026 China Open announced its main-draw wildcards on Sept 21. Olympic champion Zheng Qinwen and US Open junior champion Sun Xinyan headline the eight women's singles wildcards, while Zhang Zhizhen, Shang Juncheng and Bu Yunchaokete take three of the four men's singles wildcards. The WTA1000/ATP500 event runs Sept 28-Oct 11 in Beijing.
