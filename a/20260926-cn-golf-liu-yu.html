Bogey-free 64 puts Liu Yu one off the lead in Arkansas

Liu Yu opened the Walmart NW Arkansas Championship with a bogey-free 64 to sit one shot behind Japan's Yuna Nishimura (63) after round one on 25 September. The Beijing native, who said she had 'worked very hard' during a three-week break after poor form, birdied six times at Pinnacle Country Club. Fellow Chinese players Liu Yiyi (67), Zhang Yahui (70) and He Muni (71) also featured in the 144-player field.
