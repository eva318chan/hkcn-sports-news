Siobhan Haughey defends women's 100m freestyle crown at Asian Games

Hong Kong star Siobhan Haughey defended her Asian Games women's 100m freestyle title with a winning time of 52.45 seconds. China's Yu Yiting took silver in 53.21 and Cheng Yujie bronze in 53.47. All three podium places went to swimmers from China and Hong Kong.
