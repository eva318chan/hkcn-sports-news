Wu Qingfeng Outswims Haughey in 50m Free as China Rakes in Four More Golds

Wu Qingfeng won the women's 50m freestyle at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sep 24 in 23.98 seconds, a new Games record and her first sub-24-second swim. Hong Kong's Siobhan Haughey took silver in 24.32 while Cheng Yujie earned bronze in 24.46. China won four golds and three bronzes in the pool that day, including a new Asian record of 3:10.60 in the men's 4x100m freestyle relay.
