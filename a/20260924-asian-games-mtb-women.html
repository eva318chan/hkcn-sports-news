China One-Two in Women's MTB Cross-Country at Nagoya Asian Games

China's Wu Zhifan won gold and Liang Zhenglan took silver in the women's mountain bike cross-country final at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sep 24, completing a 1-2 sweep for the Chinese cycling team. The result underlined China's growing strength in Asian off-road cycling.
