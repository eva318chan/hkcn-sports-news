Wang Yanwen's Hat-Trick Fires China Past Philippines and Into Asian Games Quarter-Finals

China's women thrashed the Philippines 5-1 in their final Asian Games group G match on September 21, with 王妍雯 scoring a hat-trick in the 20th, 40th and 54th minutes. 陳巧珠 and 袁叢 added the other goals, though the win came at a cost as 張琳艷 limped off injured in the 19th minute. Two wins and a draw gave China seven points and top spot in the group, sealing a quarter-final berth.
