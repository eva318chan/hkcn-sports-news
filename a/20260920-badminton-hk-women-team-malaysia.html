Hong Kong women's badminton team swept 0-3 by Malaysia at Asian Games

Hong Kong's women's badminton team were beaten 0-3 by Malaysia in the Asian Games women's team round of 16. Lo Sin Yan lost to world No.28 Letshanaa, the world No.22 pair Yeung Nga Ting/Yeung Pui Lam fell to world No.5 Pearly Tan/Thinaah after leading 13-7 in game two, and Leung Ka Wing lost to Wong Ling Ching.
