Hong Kong Bulls Storm to Undefeated Tongxin Cup Title Without Any Foreign Stars

Hong Kong Bulls won the third Fujian 闽港澳台 'Tongxin Cup' invitational with a perfect 3-0 record despite playing without foreign players, beating Macau's 昊骏天马, Taiwan Beer and CBA side Fujian. New center 区俊炫 averaged 14.3 points and was named MVP of the men's pro division, while 石奎 hit clutch late threes against Fujian. The Bulls now turn to November's CBA Club Cup.
