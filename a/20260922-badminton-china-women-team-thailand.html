China's women cruise past Thailand 3-0 into Asian Games badminton semis

China's women's badminton team opened their Asian Games campaign with a 3-0 quarterfinal win over Thailand. Wang Zhiyi came from a game down to beat Ratchanok Intanon, world No.1 pair Liu Shengshu/Tan Ning dominated the doubles, and Chen Yufei avenged her World Championships defeat to Lalinrat Chaiwan in three games.
