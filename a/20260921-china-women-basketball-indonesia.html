China Women Rout Indonesia 110-41 to Lock Up Asian Games Group Top Seed

China's women's basketball team crushed Indonesia 110-41 in the Asian Games group stage to secure first place in Group A. 张子宇 had 22 points and 5 rebounds, 刘禹彤 posted a 20-point, 10-rebound double-double, and 罗欣棫 added 17 points and 6 assists. In other action, Korea beat Mongolia 88-61 and Chinese Taipei downed Malaysia 98-59 for their second straight win.
