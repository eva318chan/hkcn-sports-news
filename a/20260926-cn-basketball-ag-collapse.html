From four golds to zero: China's basketball endures its darkest Asian Games in history

China's four basketball teams all missed the final in Nagoya: the men's 5x5 side lost 77-97 to Japan in the semifinal and 70-79 to Iran in the bronze game to finish fourth, while the women's team fell 80-85 to South Korea with a stunning 0-for-13 from three, missing the Asian Games final for the first time in 32 years. Both 3x3 squads also bowed out in the quarterfinals. Just eight years after sweeping all four golds at Jakarta 2018, Chinese basketball's slide has sparked calls for a full rebuild of the youth system, league competitiveness and playing philosophy.
