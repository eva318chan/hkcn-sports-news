Eight crowns to fourth place: China's men crumble and the Zhou Qi selection storm erupts

China's men, eight-time Asian Games champions since 1974, finished fourth in Nagoya after a record 20-point semifinal loss to Japan (77-97) and a 70-79 bronze-game defeat to Iran, their second-worst finish ever. Selection controversy flared as multiple media figures confirmed Zhou Qi volunteered to play but was left off by coach Guo Shiqiang for missing summer camp, even as the also-injured Yu Jiahao was picked, while Xu Jie reportedly met the same fate. The basketball federation has pledged a full review of selection and the wider system, from scouting to tactics.
