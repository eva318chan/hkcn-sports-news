Li Haotong and Dou Zecheng finish top ten in first PGA Tour fall event, climb world rankings

Li Haotong and Dou Zecheng both finished inside the top ten at the inaugural Biltmore Championship, the first event of the PGA Tour fall series. Dou tied seventh for his first top ten of the season, rising from 191st to 174th in the world rankings, while Li's career-low 61 in round three carried him to a tie for tenth and lifted him from 122nd to 115th. Their FedEx Cup points rankings improved to 111th and 121st respectively.
