Champions of Jiangxi: Nanchang Beat Jiujiang 2-1 in Front of Record 61,000-Strong Crowd

Nanchang won the 2026 Jiangxi City Football Super League title on 26 September, beating Jiujiang 2-1 in the final at Nanchang International Sports Center. Xiawei's 37th-minute penalty and Deng Tianao's stoppage-time strike put Nanchang 2-0 up, and Jiujiang's late reply in added time was not enough. The 61,026 attendance set a new Jiangxi football record, with over 22,000 Jiujiang fans making the away trip.
