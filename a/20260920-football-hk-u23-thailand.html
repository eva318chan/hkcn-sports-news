Hong Kong U23's Dream Start Crushed in 1-5 Asian Games Defeat to Thailand

Hong Kong U23 stunned Thailand with 胡晉銘's 13th-minute opener in their Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games group match on September 20. The lead lasted only until half-time before Thailand scored four times after the break to complete a 5-1 rout. It was Hong Kong's second straight defeat after an opening 0-2 loss to Japan, leaving their knockout hopes all but over.
