CBA Breaks Its 20-Year Closed Shop: Promotion-Relegation Playoffs Return in 2027

The Chinese Basketball Association has formally approved the return of promotion-relegation between the CBA and NBL in 2027, ending a closed-league system in place since 2005. After the 2026-27 regular season, the CBA's 19th and 20th teams will face the NBL's runners-up and champions in home-and-away playoffs (April 15-21, 2027), with winners still needing to pass CBA entry assessment. Clubs with salary arrears over three months lose playoff eligibility and are relegated automatically.
