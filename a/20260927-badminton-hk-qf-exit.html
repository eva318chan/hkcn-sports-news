Hong Kong's Badminton Run Ends at Quarter-Finals: Ng and 'Yeung Yeung Pair' Bow Out

Hong Kong's badminton campaign at the Asian Games ended in the quarter-finals on September 27. Four-time Asian Games representative Angus Ng took the first game off world No.2 Kunlavut Vitidsarn before falling 21-16, 10-21, 13-21 in the men's singles. The women's doubles pair Yeung Nga-ting and Yeung Pui-lam also lost a three-game thriller 15-21, 21-19, 15-21 to Japan's Nakanishi Takahara and Uesaka Suzu.
