Tenth crown complete: China's volleyball queens sweep Japan and set sights on LA 2028

China's women beat hosts Japan 3-0 (25-19, 25-20, 25-23) in the Nagoya final on September 22, securing a record tenth Asian Games gold and a third straight title just 22 days after losing the Asian Championship final to Thailand and missing direct Olympic qualification. Star attacker Wu Mengjie torched Japan for 26 points, and the team — averaging under 23 years old with veterans Gong Xiangyu and Wang Mengjie as the only holdovers — roared back from 2-10 down in the third set. The young squad now targets the 2027 World Cup and world rankings for a Los Angeles 2028 ticket.
