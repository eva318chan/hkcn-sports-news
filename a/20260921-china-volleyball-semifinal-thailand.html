Revenge Complete: China Outlast Thailand in Five-Set Epic to Reach Asian Games Final

China beat Thailand 3-2 (23-25, 25-21, 25-11, 28-26, 15-6) in the Asian Games semifinal, avenging the five-set loss to the same opponents in the Asian Championship final weeks earlier. Captain Gong Xiangyu came off the bench from the second set to steady the team, and China dominated the decider 15-6. Thailand had won the Asian Championship and the direct Los Angeles Olympics berth.
