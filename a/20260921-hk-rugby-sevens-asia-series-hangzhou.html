Hong Kong Men Down Japan 31-12 to Win Asia Sevens Leg; Women Take Bronze Over Thailand

Hong Kong's men completed an unbeaten weekend at the Asia Rugby Sevens Series leg in Hangzhou, thrashing Japan 31-12 in the final with tries from Bryn Phillips, Harry Sayers, Sebastian Brien, 房傑鋒 and Liam Herbert. Earlier they routed Uzbekistan 34-0, the UAE 54-7, Malaysia 59-0 and Sri Lanka 33-0 in the semifinal. The women took bronze, beating Thailand 19-17 twice with Julia Oyana scoring a hat-trick in the playoff.
