Historic First: FIFA ASEAN Cup Kicks Off in Hong Kong as Laos Down Brunei 3-1

The inaugural FIFA ASEAN Cup Division 2 tournament opened at Hong Kong's Mong Kok Stadium, with Laos beating Brunei 3-1 in the opener. Fourteen teams are taking part, with the Hong Kong leg featuring China Hong Kong, Laos, Brunei, Myanmar, Cambodia and Timor-Leste from 24 September to 3 October. China Hong Kong, who sat out the opening day, face Laos on 27 September and Brunei on 30 September at the Kai Tak Stadium, with the group winners contesting the final on 3 October.
