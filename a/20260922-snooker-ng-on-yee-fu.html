Ng On-yee whitewashes Fu Ka-chun 4-0 in Hong Kong derby at Scottish Open qualifiers

Hong Kong's Ng On-yee swept compatriot Fu Ka-chun 4-0 in a one-sided Hong Kong derby during the Scottish Open qualifying rounds in Leicester on September 21. Wang Xinbo, Liu Wenwei, Lan Yuhao and Zhao Hanyang also won on the day, including a 4-3 comeback victory for Liu over Andrew Higginson. Across the first qualifying round, eight of China's 16 entrants advanced.
