Wang Chuqin's double rescues China as Lin Shidong suffers shock loss to Thai teen

China's men's table tennis team beat Thailand 3-1 in the Asian Games quarterfinals to reach the semis against Chinese Taipei. Wang Chuqin won both his singles without dropping a game, but Lin Shidong suffered a stunning 2-3 defeat to 16-year-old Thai player Puritiya after twice leading, with Xiang Peng securing the third point.
