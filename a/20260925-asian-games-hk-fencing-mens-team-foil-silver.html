Teen Anchor Steps In as Hong Kong's Foil Men Take Team Silver at Asian Games

The Hong Kong men's foil team of Cheung Ka Long, Ryan Choi, Leung Chin Yu and Ho Shing Him lost 36-45 to host Japan in the Asian Games team final at the Aichi International Exhibition Centre on 25 September. Coach Greg boldly switched 17-year-old Ho Shing Him to the anchor position after Ryan Choi volunteered to step down, and the youngster briefly closed the gap to two points with a nine-touch leg. The silver equals Hong Kong's best team result set in 2018 and marks a sixth consecutive Asian Games team medal, earning the squad a HK$1 million Jockey Club incentive award.
