Zheng Qinwen touches down in Beijing as China Open countdown begins

Zheng Qinwen arrived in Beijing on 25 September to prepare for the China Open, where she will play as a wild card for the fourth straight year. The 2024 semi-finalist trained twice a day at the National Tennis Center's Diamond Court and took time to try calligraphy and the guqin, looking relaxed ahead of the 28 September start. World No.1 Aryna Sabalenka leads a field featuring 11 Grand Slam champions, and Zheng hopes home support can lift her past her career-best Beijing run.
