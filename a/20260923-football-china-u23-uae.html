China U23 Grind Out Fiery Goalless Draw With UAE to Top Asian Games Group

China U23 drew 0-0 with the UAE in their final Asian Games group B match in Nagoya on September 23, finishing top of the group with five points from one win and two draws. Goalkeeper 李昊 made several crucial saves, including a key stop in the 78th minute, as tempers flared twice during the match. Forward 王钰栋 picked up a second yellow card of the tournament and will miss the quarter-final, where China face Thailand on September 26.
