Hong Kong's Sevens Squads Named: Men Hunt Asian Games Three-Peat

Hong Kong China Rugby has confirmed its men's and women's sevens squads for the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, where rugby sevens runs Oct 1-3. The men, captained by James Christie with veteran Cado Lee at his fourth Games, chase a third straight Asian Games gold fresh from beating Japan to win the Asia Rugby Sevens Series leg in Suzhou. The women, led by captain Chloe Chan with eight Hangzhou bronze medallists returning, aim to improve on their historic podium finish.
