Gold and Silver Secured: Lin/Kuai Stun World No.1 Koreans to Set Up All-Chinese Final

China's young pair Lin Shidong and Kuai Man fought back from the brink to beat world No.1 Koreans Lim Jong-hoon and Shin Yu-bin 4-2 in the mixed doubles semi-finals on September 25. Trailing 2-3 in games and 8-10 in the fifth, they saved two game points to win 12-10 and closed out 11-3. With Wang Chuqin/Sun Yingsha also advancing, China locked up gold and silver before the final.
