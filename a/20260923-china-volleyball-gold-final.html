China Sweep Hosts Japan for Asian Games Volleyball Gold, Historic Third Straight Crown

China's young women's volleyball team beat hosts Japan 3-0 (25-19, 25-20, 25-23) in the Nagoya Asian Games final, completing an Asian Games three-peat and a record-extending tenth title. After falling 1-7 (and 2-10) behind in the third set, China stormed back to win 25-23, with Wu Mengjie scoring a match-high 26 points. The win capped a 22-day turnaround after losing to Thailand in the Asian Championship final.
