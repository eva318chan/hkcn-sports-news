China Men Fall 70-79 to Iran in Bronze Match, End Asian Games Medal-Less in Fourth

China lost 70-79 to Iran in the Asian Games bronze-medal game, finishing fourth and missing the podium — the team's second-worst Asian Games result ever after 2014's fifth place. China led 31-29 at halftime, but a 13-4 Iran run in the third quarter turned the game; a late rally pulled China within 62-63 before Iran closed it out. Pang Zhenglin scored a game-high 22 points for China.
