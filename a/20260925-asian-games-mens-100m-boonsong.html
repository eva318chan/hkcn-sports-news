Thailand's Boonsong Smashes Su Bingtian's Asian Games 100m Record — Twice in One Day

Thai 20-year-old Puripol Boonsong clocked 9.91 in the men's 100m semi-finals at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sep 25, breaking Su Bingtian's 8-year-old 9.92 Games record, then lowered it again to 9.90 to win the final that same evening. The mark is the second-fastest in Asian history behind Su's 9.83 Asian record, and Boonsong went sub-10 in all three of his runs at the Games. China's Zeng Keli and Xie Zhenye finished sixth and seventh.
