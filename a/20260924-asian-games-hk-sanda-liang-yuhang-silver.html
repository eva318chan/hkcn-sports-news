Injured-in-Secret Sanda Star Stuns Debut with Asian Games Silver

Hong Kong sanda fighter Leung Yu-hang took silver in the men's 60kg final on 24 September at the Aichi Prefectural Budokan, losing by WPD (points-disparity victory) to China's Tang Sisuo, the reigning National Games champion. The 21-year-old former world champion revealed afterwards he had hidden a right-foot injury for two weeks before the Games. It is Hong Kong sanda's best Asian Games result ever and the squad's second medal of these Games after Chan Tsz-ching's women's 52kg bronze, earning Leung a HK$500,000 Jockey Club award.
