Ka Ying Rising lands in Sydney to begin The Everest title defence

Hong Kong superstar Ka Ying Rising arrived safely in Sydney on Monday morning after departing Sha Tin on Sunday night to defend his title in the A$20 million The Everest at Randwick on October 17. The David Hayes-trained six-year-old is now in quarantine at Canterbury Park, accompanied by retired stablemate Ka Ying Glory, and will trial at Randwick on October 6. He carries a 21-race winning streak and a world-best rating of 135 into the world's richest turf race.
