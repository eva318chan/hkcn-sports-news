Eight Chinese players through as Liu Hongyu stuns Jimmy White in Scottish Open qualifying

Eight Chinese players qualified for the 2026 Scottish Open main event after the final qualifying day in Leicester on 23 September. Liu Hongyu came from 2-3 down to beat 10-time ranking champion Jimmy White 4-3, while Fan Zhengyi, Lyu Haotian, Zhao Hanyang and Xu Si also won through, joining Yao Pengcheng, Jiang Jun and Gao Yang. Ding Junhui opted out, leaving him the only one of 34 Chinese players to skip the event.
