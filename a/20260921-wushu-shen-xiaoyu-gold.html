Shen Xiaoyu wins Hong Kong's first gold of the Asian Games in women's changquan

Shen Xiaoyu scored 9.713 points to win the women's changquan event at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, giving the Hong Kong delegation its first gold of the Games. The flag bearer was competing at her third Asian Games and entered only the changquan event. She previously won the wushu title at the 2025 Chengdu World Games and finished runner-up at this year's Wushu Taolu World Cup.
