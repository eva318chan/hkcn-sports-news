Hong Kong's men bow out 0-3 to South Korea in table tennis team quarters

Hong Kong's men's table tennis team lost 0-3 to South Korea in the Asian Games quarterfinals, extending their last-eight exit streak to five editions. Chan Ho Wah fell to world No.11 Jang Woojin, Wong Chun Ting lost in three close games to Oh Junsung, and Lam Siu Hang was beaten 1-3 by An Jaehyun, ending Hong Kong's team campaign.
