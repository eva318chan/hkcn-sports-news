Blue Derby Fireworks: Eastern Come From Behind to Stun Kitchee 3-1 in League Cup Opener

Eastern came from behind to beat Kitchee 3-1 in the 'Blue Derby' League Cup opener at Tai Po Sports Ground, the first match of the season's new double-elimination format. Kitchee, fielding a young side, struck within the first minute through Adrian, but Eastern replied with strikes from Marcos and two from Yuto Okubo before half-time. A second-half red card for Kitchee's Yu Ching-wai ended any comeback hopes, sending Eastern into the winners' bracket second round and Kitchee to the losers' bracket.
