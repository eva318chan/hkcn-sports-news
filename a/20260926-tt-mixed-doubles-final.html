Young Guns Dethrone 'Shatou': Lin Shidong/Kuai Man Sweep to Asian Games Mixed Doubles Gold

Lin Shidong and Kuai Man stunned defending champions Wang Chuqin and Sun Yingsha 4-0 in the all-Chinese mixed doubles final on September 26, ending the 'Shatou' pair's bid for a third straight Asian Games title. Every game was decided by just two points (11-9, 13-11, 11-9, 11-9), with the youngsters completing a remarkable comeback from 2-8 down in the fourth. It was China's sixth time sweeping the event's gold and silver, and a clear passing of the torch.
