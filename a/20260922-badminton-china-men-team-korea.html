China's men edge South Korea 3-2 in five-match thriller to reach Asian Games semis

China's men's badminton team survived a five-match battle against South Korea 3-2 in the Asian Games quarterfinals. Shi Yuqi came from a game down to win the opener, He Jiting/Liu Yi — a last-minute pairing after Chen Baiyang's illness withdrawal — won the decisive doubles, and Weng Hongyang sealed it 21-17, 21-15 in the final singles.
