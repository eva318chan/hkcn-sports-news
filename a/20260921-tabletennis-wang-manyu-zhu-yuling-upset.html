World No.1 Wang Manyu stunned by Macau's Zhu Yuling in Asian Games opener

Newly-crowned world No.1 Wang Manyu suffered a shock 1-3 defeat to former world No.1 Zhu Yuling, now representing Macau, in the Asian Games women's team group stage on Sept 20 — China's first loss of the table tennis tournament. Sun Yingsha then won twice, including an 11-1 final game against Zhu Yuling, as China beat Macau 3-1 to top the group.
