China sweeps debut half-marathon race walking titles at Asian Games

China swept the men's and women's titles in the Asian Games debut of the half-marathon race walk over 21.0975km. Nineteen-year-old Shi Shengji won the men's race in 1:23:32, the 200th Asian Games gold in Chinese athletics history, while Yang Liujing took the women's title in 1:33:40 with Jiang Yunyan earning bronze.
