Lin Yun-Ju Makes History: First Asian Games Men's Singles Semi-Final in 12 Years

Taiwan's Lin Yun-Ju defeated Hong Kong's 21-year-old Chan Ho-wa 4-1 in the men's singles quarter-finals on September 26, securing at least a bronze and his first Asian Games singles medal. It was Taiwan's first men's singles semi-final appearance in 12 years, since Chuang Chih-yuan in 2014. Lin will next face China's Lin Shidong for a place in the gold-medal match.
