Guo Li wins Asian Games women's road race in historic first for Chinese cycling

China's Guo Li claimed the Asian Games women's road race gold medal in 2 hours 50 minutes and 5 seconds. It was the Chinese delegation's 24th gold of the Games and the first time the Chinese cycling team has won this event. The result marks a historic breakthrough for Chinese road cycling.
