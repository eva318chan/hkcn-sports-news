Midnight Badminton Farce: Sindhu-Miyazaki Tie Starts at 12:08 AM in Empty Nagoya Arena

India's P.V. Sindhu beat Japan's Tomoka Miyazaki 2-0 in the women's singles third round, but the match didn't start until 12:08 AM on September 27 after a day of schedule overruns. The arena was nearly empty by then, with shuttle buses already stopped, sparking outrage from journalists and fans over the organizers' scheduling. The 41-minute match finished at 12:49 AM, drawing sharp criticism of Nagoya's Games management.
