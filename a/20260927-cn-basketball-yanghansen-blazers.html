Blazers insider flags Yang Hansen's troubling 42% three-point diet: stop chucking, start driving

Portland media personality Mike Richmond said on the Locked On Blazers podcast that 42% of Yang Hansen's shots last season came from beyond the arc, and his 11.9% three-point rate was the worst among all 2.13m-plus players with that shot diet. Richmond argued Yang's slow release and excessive threes waste his size and interior touch, urging him to cut down perimeter attempts and attack the rim instead. He also rated Yang as the fifth center on the depth chart, unlikely to crack the opening-day rotation, and suggested an early G League stint to develop.
