Comeback Gymnast Ends Hong Kong's Eight-Year Wait for an Asian Games Medal

Hong Kong gymnast Ng Ka Ki scored an average of 14.116 in the men's vault final on 25 September to win bronze, his first Asian Games medal and Hong Kong gymnastics' first since Shek Wai Hung's back-to-back vault golds in 2014 and 2018. The 27-year-old, who tore his calf muscle in February but returned at the Asian Championships to book his ticket, finished behind double Olympic champion Carlos Yulo (14.599) and Iran's Mahdi Olfati (14.533). The bronze carries a HK$250,000 Jockey Club incentive award.
