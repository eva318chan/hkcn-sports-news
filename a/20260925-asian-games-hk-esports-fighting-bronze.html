Hong Kong's Fighting-Game Trio Opens Esports Medal Account with Asian Games Bronze

Hong Kong's Wong Yuk-cheung, Yip Man-ho and Lee Hin-hei took bronze in the Asian Games esports "Competitive Martial Arts" team event (Street Fighter 6, Tekken 8 and The King of Fighters XV) on 25 September after a 0-3 semi-final defeat to host Japan, with no bronze-medal playoff. The trio had edged Thailand 3-2 in the quarter-finals to secure the medal, Hong Kong's first in esports at these Games, worth a HK$500,000 Jockey Club award. Debutant Lee Hin-hei celebrated on camera with a shout-out to his mother: "Mum, I did it!"
