Hong Kong U23 Bow Out of Asian Games Winless After 4-0 Defeat to Kyrgyzstan

Hong Kong U23 ended their Asian Games campaign with a 4-0 defeat to Kyrgyzstan in Kariya on September 23. Goalkeeper 潘尚希's seventh-minute handling error opened the floodgates, with further goals in the 22nd, 63rd and 86th minutes. Three defeats from three, with one goal scored and eleven conceded, left Hong Kong bottom of the group.
